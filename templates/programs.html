Annual Programs

Welcome & Farewell Program

A structured annual event welcoming new students and bidding farewell to graduating seniors. Includes networking, entertainment, and recognition of achievements.

Held: 2 times/year
Internship Orientation Program

Collaboration with hospitals, NGOs, and health tech companies. Provides internship preparation, connects students with opportunities, and facilitates placements.

Held: Before internship season
Career Counselling & Development

Regular workshops covering CV building, resume writing, LinkedIn profiles, interview preparation, certifications, and career guidance from professionals.

Held: Monthly/Quarterly
Publications Unit

Annual magazine and semi-annual wall magazine. Showcases student work, research highlights, club achievements, and health informatics insights.

Held: Annually & Semi-annually
Annual Symposium

Academic symposium focusing on digital health and health informatics topics. Features expert talks, paper presentations, and discussions on emerging trends.

Held: Annually
Health Informatics Meet

Flagship national event promoting digital health networking. Brings together students, professionals, and organizations for knowledge sharing and collaboration.

Held: Annually
